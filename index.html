Invisible Cello

Stand back so both hands are in view. Right hand bows: sweep it side to side, and raise or lower it to change string (A at the top, C at the bottom). Left hand fingers: slide it down the neck to raise the pitch. Pinch thumb and index together to stop the note. Lean your bow hand toward the camera to dig in. Run Calibrate once so the cello fits your reach.
